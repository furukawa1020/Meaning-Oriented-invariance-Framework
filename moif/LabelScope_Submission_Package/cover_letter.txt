Dear Editor-in-Chief,

I am submitting my manuscript, “LabelScope: A Claim-Capping Framework for Auditing Operational Labels in Physiology-Based Human-Machine Systems,” for consideration as a Regular Paper in IEEE Transactions on Human-Machine Systems.

This manuscript addresses a methodological problem in physiology-based human-machine systems: high predictive performance on operational labels is often treated as sufficient support for broader internal-state claims. In closed-loop or adaptive HMS, this can create a mismatch between what a model predicts and what kind of system action can be defensibly justified.

The primary contribution of this paper is LabelScope, a claim-capping framework for auditing whether an operational label can support an intended HMS state claim. Rather than focusing only on classifier performance, LabelScope evaluates the supportability of the claim through three audit modules: Resolution Audit, Proxy Audit, and Structure Audit. The framework outputs an interpretive claim level that constrains how predictive results should be reported and how they may be connected to adaptive system behavior.

The framework is demonstrated through retrospective case studies on the WESAD and SWELL-KW datasets. These case studies show that high predictive accuracy can coexist with restrictive claim caps, especially when operational labels are affected by resolution mismatch, behavioral-proxy risk, or limited physiological structure support.

I believe the manuscript is well aligned with IEEE Transactions on Human-Machine Systems because it contributes a methodological framework for more cautious and reproducible interpretation of physiological sensing results in adaptive HMS. By shifting the question from “how accurately can we predict this label?” to “what HMS claim can this label defensibly support?”, LabelScope aims to strengthen the methodological basis of physiology-based human-machine system design.

This manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration elsewhere.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,
